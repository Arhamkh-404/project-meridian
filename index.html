Story Summarized

A chronological overview of Aayan's journey, the silent world, and the ultimate sacrifice.

01. The Ambition & Secret Lab

Driven by a childhood question about the future, Nobel laureate Aayan builds an underground laboratory beneath an abandoned warehouse, constructing the Meridian—a capsule designed to bend spacetime using an artificial black hole managed by an AI named Blix.

03. The Leap & Silent World

Aayan jumps 150 years into the future. He emerges into a chillingly silent world: hollow skyscrapers, broken roads, and rusted cars under a grey sky, with no human life remaining.

05. The Archive & Sacrifice

An emergency terminal reveals that humanity weaponized his early quantum research, triggering World War III. Racing back, he fixes the broken return switch, erases the fatal file, and sacrifices his life so the world can live on.
