Logline & Synopsis

Driven by a childhood question about the future, Nobel laureate Aayan Rehman builds the Meridian—an underground capsule designed to bend spacetime using an artificial black hole. When he leaps 150 years ahead, he finds a desolate, silent Earth destroyed by nuclear war born from his own early research. Returning to his own time, he executes a final deletion protocol, sacrificing his own life to erase the weaponized equations and preserve humanity's future.
