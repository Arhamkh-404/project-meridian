The Meridian Chronicles

The complete chronological log of Aayan's leap, the silent world, and the final sacrifice.

01. The Ambition

Aayan Rehman was the kind of student teachers remember. He earned his electronics degree at Dawood University, went on to a PhD at MIT, and later won a Nobel Prize in physics for research that changed how the world understood energy, matter, and time. Yet praise made him uneasy. The prize sat among his books while he focused on a childhood question: what will the world look like when we are gone? Every discovery he made was a step toward seeing that answer with his own eyes.

02. The Secret Laboratory

Beneath an abandoned warehouse, behind an unmarked hatch, Aayan spent years constructing a subterranean facility. Lit by cyan strips and operated by robotic arms, every wall was covered in equations. At the center stood his life’s work: the Meridian, a sealed capsule designed to bend spacetime through the gravity of an artificial black hole. Beside him was Blix, a lab AI existing as a floating ring of blue light. When a breakthrough equation snapped into place one night, Aayan handed Blix full operational control.

03. The Leap

Strapped into the capsule, Aayan pulled the primary lever as Blix confirmed parameters. A flash of blue swallowed the lab, followed by total darkness and a violent rush toward a black hole whose glowing accretion disk bent starlight into streaks. Galaxies spun and counters raced upward as 150 years fell away in seconds. When the capsule stopped, Aayan was shaking and laughing—his lifelong theory was real.

04. The Silent World

The Meridian powered down with a sharp click, but the lab was unrecognizable: yellowed papers, dust, and cracked ceilings dripping stale water. Stepping onto the surface, there was no sound—no traffic, no voices, no signals—only a thin wind. Hollow skyscrapers stood like skeletons while vines broke through cracked roads. A dry cough caught in Aayan's throat as he stood alone under a grey sky.

05. The Archive

Searching half-collapsed government ruins, Aayan connected a spare power cell to a cracked terminal. Red warning holograms flickered to life, showing missile launches, mushroom clouds, and burning flags. A calm voice explained that world military powers had taken Aayan’s early quantum energy research and built weapons that caused World War III. Seeing his own equations beside the devastation, Aayan sank to his knees, unaware that nuclear fallout was filling his lungs.

06. The Broken Switch

Determined to make sure his research was never weaponized, Aayan ran back through empty streets to the Meridian's console. He entered the return sequence, but the emergency switch was dead—its panel cracked and circuits burnt. Panicking, he remembered his foundational training as an electronics engineer. He bridged the burnt connections with scrap wire until a faint indicator lit up, then slammed the switch.

07. The Return

A blinding white flash brought Aayan back to his warm, humming lab, exactly as he had left it. Behind him, the Meridian sparked and died permanently. Without stopping to explain to Blix, Aayan opened the server, located the fatal research project, and permanently erased the files. The weapons would never be built, and World War III would never happen.

08. The Last Breath

Aayan leaned back in his chair, at peace. But as his chest tightened and his arms grew heavy,his vision blurred, he realized the radioactive fallout from the ruined future had already poisoned his lungs.He smiled faintly knowing the world was safe. His hand slid from the armrest as he collapses from the chair,the lights starts to go off,as he collapses while the world outside carried on unaware of his sacrifice.
