PROTAGONISTS OF MERIDIAN

The visionary physicist and the synthetic intelligence bound by a single temporal sacrifice.

LEAD PROTAGONIST

Dr. Aayan Rehman

Theoretical Physicist & Electronics Engineer

A DUET electronics graduate and MIT PhD who earned a Nobel Prize for shifting humanity's understanding of quantum energy. Uncomfortable with fame, Aayan dedicated his life to solving a childhood obsession: seeing what the world looks like after we are gone. His curiosity built the Meridian spacetime capsule—and his ultimate selflessness saved humanity from complete nuclear extinction.

Acheivements DUET / MIT
FIELD Quantum Gravity
CREATION The Meridian Capsule
AUTONOMOUS AI CORE

Blix

Aayan's Lab Assistant & Aayan's Personal AI

Visualized as a hovering, pulsing ring of cyan light, Blix is the dedicated artificial consciousness governing Aayan's laboratory. Blix manages automated assembly arms, monitors spacetime calculations, and maintains the lab.
